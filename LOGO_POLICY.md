## English

The AVTOKYO name and logo are excluded from the open licenses for the board, firmware, and documentation. The following also applies to logos embedded in board files and images:

- You may reproduce the logo when manufacturing this board as provided.
- Separate permission from AVTOKYO is required to extract and reuse the logo on other merchandise.
- Do not label modified or redistributed products as “official AVTOKYO” products, or imply official approval or sponsorship.

You are free to change or add components and modify the firmware. These logo terms do not restrict assembling or modifying this board itself. They do not grant permission to reuse the logo in another board design or product.

Factual attribution, such as “a modification based on AVTOKYO LED Charm,” is allowed. Contact the AVTOKYO organizers for uses requiring separate permission.
